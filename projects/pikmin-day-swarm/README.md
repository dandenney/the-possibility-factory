# Pikmin Day Swarm

Lead a swarm of sprouts around a lawn, throw them at crumbs, and carry everything home to the onion, which pops
out more sprouts. A tiny fan toy for Pikmin Day (November 1). Not affiliated with Nintendo.

Live: https://pikmin-day-swarm.netlify.app

- Move (or drag) to lead. Click (or tap) to throw. Double-click to charge. Hold Space or Whistle to call them back.
- Number keys pick a type to throw; X dismisses; C charges.
- Red are all-rounders, yellow fly farthest, blue can wade into puddles, purple carry like four, white are fast.
- Type a line like "20 tiny pink speedy swimmers" to invent a type. Share the link and your types come with it.
