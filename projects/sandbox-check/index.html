      Test your own app

      Copy the probe as a standalone HTML page and get it rendered in your preview feature: paste it into your app, or ask your model to output it verbatim. It reports from inside the frame what it can reach. Nothing in it changes the host page; the only requests it makes are for a tiny image on this site, to test whether data can get out.

      The short checklist

      • Never combine allow-scripts with allow-same-origin on srcdoc or blob URLs.
      • Best of all, serve previews from a separate origin that has no cookies, storage or APIs worth stealing.
      • The sandbox doesn't stop requests going out. A CSP inside the frame does.
      • srcdoc and blob frames inherit your page's CSP, so test with your real headers.
      • Every message listener checks event.source. event.origin is "null" for every sandboxed frame.
      • Leave out allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox, allow-top-navigation and an allow list you don't need.

      Every probe is harmless: it reads but never writes, installs nothing and only requests files from this site. Prompted by Parameter Labs' write-up on account takeover through ChatGPT's HTML previews. Made by The Possibility Factory.
