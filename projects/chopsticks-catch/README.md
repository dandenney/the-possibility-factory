# Chopsticks Catch

Bring a rocket booster home to the launch tower and catch it in the chopstick arms. One button: hold to burn, let
go in the catch window to close the arms. Then share a replay GIF of your catch or crash. A fan toy, not
affiliated with SpaceX.

Live: https://chopsticks-catch.netlify.app

- Hold Space, the mouse or your finger to burn. The engines also steer you back toward the arms against the wind.
- Let go once the pins drop into the orange catch window. Slower and more centered scores higher.
- You get 8 seconds of propellant. Every flight number has its own wind; share a link and a friend gets the same flight.
