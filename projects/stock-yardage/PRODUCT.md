# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dan and Misty, a couple newly into golf, logging shots on a phone between swings at GOLFTEC, a Topgolf bay, a
driving range or on the course, then checking the card before picking a club.

## Product Purpose

Personal stock yardages that don't depend on whose measurement you trust: the last five good shots per club, tee
and no tee, and how each venue's numbers compare with the course.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, launch-monitor. The same "Ball Flight" identity as dandenney.com/golf.

## Anti-references

Generic golf apps with green turf textures, scorecard skeuomorphism, ads and subscriptions. Dashboards with many
competing colors.

## Design Principles

- One-handed and glanceable: big tap targets, numbers first.
- Tracer chartreuse is the ball (and tee shots); cyan only separates no-tee; amber only for mishits.
- Mono for every number, Space Grotesk for words.
- Nothing leaves the phone without the user pressing Export.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on the dark bay, visible focus, reduced motion honored, usable in bright sun at high brightness.
