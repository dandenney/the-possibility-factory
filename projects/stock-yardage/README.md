# Stock Yardage

How far do you actually hit your 7 iron? GOLFTEC, Topgolf and the range signs all give different answers. Log your good shots by club, on and off a tee, wherever you hit them, and Stock Yardage keeps a card of your last five good ones per club: low, typical and high. A per-club view shows which venue reads long against the course.

Live: https://stock-yardage.netlify.app

- Everything stays on your phone. Export a file to move it to another phone or to update dandenney.com/golf.
- Each player has their own bag, so odd sets (7 wood, 6 hybrid, 52°) fit.
- Mishits get logged but never count toward the card.
- Add it to your home screen and it works at the range with no signal.
