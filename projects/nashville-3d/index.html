Nashville in 3D

Downtown, a kilometre around the Batman Building, built from open data: lidar terrain, an aerial photo and OpenStreetMap buildings. Drag to move it, pinch or scroll to zoom.

    Where the data comes from
    • Built with OpenGeoWorld by Urban Geo Analytics (MIT), then baked into one file.
    • Buildings, roads, water and trees © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). Heights come from the map tags; missing ones are estimated.
    • Terrain: USGS 3DEP lidar. Aerial photo: USGS NAIP, 60 cm. Both public domain.
    • Facade photos, sidewalks and some trees are generic textures and guesses, not the real thing.
