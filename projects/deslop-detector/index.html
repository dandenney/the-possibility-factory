      How the score works

      Each habit is a regular expression with a weight. The score is the weighted count per 1,000 words, the same scale as the detector behind qwen3-4b-deslop, where careful human writing scores under 15 and the stock Qwen3-4B averages 121. This one is an independent take on that style guide; on the 80 sample answers in that post it averages 122 before de-slopping and 7 after (theirs: 121 and 13).

      A regex can't tell good writing from bad. Writers used em dashes and lists of three long before chatbots did, so treat a high score as a reason to reread, not proof that a model wrote it.
