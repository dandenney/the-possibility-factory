# Dry County

Scene one of a Prohibition-era management game in the spirit of Gangsters 2: the still house. Turn corn, sugar
and creek water into mash, let it ferment, run it through the copper still and fill jars of moonshine.

Live: https://dry-county.netlify.app

- Mix a batch in a barrel: 1 sack of corn, 1 sack of sugar, 10 gallons of water. It cooks for 2 hours, then
  ferments for 36.
- Pour ready mash into the still and light the fire. 6 hours later you have 6 jars.
- Run low on water? Haul 10 gallons from the creek (1 hour).
- One game hour passes every 3 seconds. Pause with `P`, `1` for normal speed, `2` for fast.

Your still house saves in this browser. Markets, buyers, deliveries and hired hands come in later scenes.

## Art styles

Two looks share the same simulation: **Painted** (lantern-lit isometric) and **Ledger** (ink on paper, one copper accent, side-on). Switch under the title, or link one directly with `?look=painted` / `?look=ledger`. The choice is saved in the browser.
