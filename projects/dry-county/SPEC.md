# Dry County: scene one spec

## Goal

Prove the first production loop of a Gangsters-style moonshine game is fun to watch and play in the browser.

## Loop

1. Start October 1, 1928, 6 AM with 4 corn, 4 sugar, 20 gal water, two empty barrels and a cold still.
2. Mix mash (1 corn, 1 sugar, 10 gal water → 10 gal mash). Cooks 2 h, ferments 36 h, then ready.
3. Pour one ready barrel into the still, light it. Runs 6 h, a jar every hour, 6 jars per run.
4. Haul water from the creek: 1 h, up to 10 gal, barrel holds 40.
5. Scene ends when every sack is used and all mash is run: 24 jars.

## Time

1 game hour = 3 real seconds at normal speed; fast is 6×; pause available.

## Look

Isometric still-house cutaway drawn in SVG: chunky, warm, lantern-lit, day/night through the window. Clickable
stations mirror a side panel of jobs, inventory and a log. Mobile stacks the room above the panel.

## Out of scope

Markets, buyers, deliveries, hiring, money, the law.
